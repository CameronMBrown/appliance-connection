# 00 — Project Brief

## The business

**The Appliance Connection** — a complete home appliance installation business owned by Craig Holmes,
serving **Durham Region** and **Peterborough**, Ontario. 30+ years in business; licensed & insured;
warranties all work; responds within 24 hours.

Scope of work: install every make/model of kitchen & laundry appliance (including high-end built-ins,
custom panels, ventilation), gas fitting (ranges, dryers, BBQs, garage/shop heaters, pool + water
heaters, above/below-grade gas piping), plumbing rough-ins & fixtures, and full kitchen/laundry
renovations from rough-in to finish.

**Retail referral partners** (local appliance stores that refer install work) get a light

## Audience & positioning

- **Primary audience: homeowners (B2C)** searching for appliance installation / gas / kitchen reno
  locally. The best lead is a homeowner mid-reno investing real money.
- **Positioning: premium & approachable craftsman.** The real differentiator isn't "cheap handyman" —
  it's 30 years, licensed/insured, and installing the high-end built-ins a general handyman won't touch.

## Goals

1. A professional, modern, personality-forward site (replacing the current site).
2. **Grow local/regional SEO reach** — rank in Durham & Peterborough towns.
3. **Convert more homeowner leads** — dual path: click-to-call + quote form.

Developer goal (parallel): learn **Astro** + deepen headless WP/GraphQL.
