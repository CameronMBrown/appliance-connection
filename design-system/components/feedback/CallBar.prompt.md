Sticky ink-black bar for tap-to-call, pinned to the bottom of the viewport on mobile.

```jsx
<CallBar label="Contact Us" numbers={[{ name: 'Durham', phone: '905·259·6545', tel: '19052596545' }]} />
```

Always solid ink (`--neutral-900`), independent of the page's light/dark section theme — it's a persistent affordance, not a content band.
