A row of checkmark credential badges — years in business, licenses, warranty.

```jsx
<TrustBar items={['30+ years', 'Licensed & insured', 'Certified plumber']} />
```

The ✓ glyph is the brand's only "icon" for this pattern (no icon set) — see the Iconography section of the readme. Only list a badge when it's a real, verifiable credential.
